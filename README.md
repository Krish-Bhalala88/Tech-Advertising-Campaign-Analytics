# 📊 Advertising Campaign Performance & ROI Analytics

> **Turn advertising data into clear business decisions with Python, SQL & Power BI.**

---

## 🚀 Project Overview

This project analyzes **10,000 advertising campaign records** to understand what drives revenue, profit, conversions, and advertising ROI.

The analysis explores **platforms, campaign objectives, audiences, devices, ad placements, and creative strategies** to identify high-performing and underperforming campaigns.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

The main goal is to answer:

- 💰 How much revenue and profit are campaigns generating?
- 📈 Which platforms have the best ROAS?
- 🎯 Which campaign objectives perform best?
- 👥 Which audience segments generate the most value?
- 📱 Which devices perform best?
- 🎨 Which creative formats and emotions work best?
- ⚠️ Which campaigns are losing money?
